Day-1 phone check for the in-store AR guide. Use Chrome on Android. Nothing leaves the phone unless you copy or share the results.

1 · Phone, camera and motion sensors

Allow the camera when asked. Takes about 6 seconds.

2 · AR tracking test

Point the phone at the floor, then move it slowly sideways for about 8 seconds. Also checks whether the camera still opens while AR is running.

3 · Camera → AR hand-off

Opens the camera, closes it, then starts AR, and times the gap. This is the moment the app goes from scanning a floor marker to showing arrows.
