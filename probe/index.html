Namma Space · device probe v1.3

Phone check for the in-store AR guide. Use Chrome on Android. Nothing leaves the phone unless you copy or share the results.

1 · Phone, camera and motion sensors

Allow the camera when asked. Takes about 6 seconds. (Already done? You can skip it.)

2 · AR frame-rate tests (A → D)

For each test: stand in a lit room, aim the phone at the floor 1–2 m ahead (a rug or tiles helps) and walk slowly forward 2–3 steps. Each test takes 10–12 seconds. Run them in order.

3 · Camera → AR hand-off

Opens the camera, closes it, starts AR and times the gap (fallback path).
